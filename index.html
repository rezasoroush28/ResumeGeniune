The Human Behind The Code

I'm Reza, a backend developer from Tehran who believes code should solve real problems, not create new ones. I graduated from Sharif University of Technology with a passion for building systems that just work.

What gets me excited? Taking messy requirements and turning them into elegant, maintainable architectures. I love the puzzle of microservices, the satisfaction of optimizing a slow query, and the art of writing APIs that other developers actually enjoy using.

My approach is simple: listen carefully, communicate clearly, and take ownership of every line I write. Whether it's architecting a search engine or debugging a production issue at midnight, I'm all in.
